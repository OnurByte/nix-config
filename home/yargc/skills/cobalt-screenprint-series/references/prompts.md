# canonical prompt recipes

Use one recipe as the base prompt. Keep the final negative constraints intact after making a requested variation.

## 01 — the tiny figure and falling sky

```text
Wide 16:9 surreal minimalist editorial artwork, a tiny black human silhouette standing alone near the bottom-center of an enormous warm-white landscape, saturated cobalt-blue sky above, thousands of thin cobalt-blue vertical and diagonal pigment trails descending from the sky and bending gently around the figure like a surreal rainfall of ink, dense stippled clusters alternating with empty areas, trails becoming longer and more fragmented toward the horizon, tiny white crescent moon partially surrounded by blue marks, strict cobalt blue, warm white and black only, rough screen-print texture, visible paper fibers, uneven ink density, imperfect hand-printed edges, flat graphic composition, quiet mysterious 1990s experimental art-book aesthetic, no circles, no rain realism, no other colors, no CGI, no 3D.
```

## 03 — the street lamp

```text
Wide 16:9 surreal minimalist editorial artwork, one tiny vintage black street lamp standing completely alone at the center of an enormous warm ivory curved horizon, the lamp perfectly crisp and rendered as a simple solid black silhouette with a classic old-fashioned lantern shape, vast saturated cobalt-blue sky occupying the upper half of the composition, enormous cobalt-blue flowing radial currents spreading outward from the lamp like invisible wind made visible, thousands of irregular stippled pigment marks and thin hand-drawn blue strokes forming sweeping organic arcs around and behind the lamp, the currents expanding dramatically toward the edges and becoming increasingly fragmented and sparse, dense blue-and-ivory pigment concentration immediately behind the lamp creating a soft circular burst without forming perfect geometric circles, long blue textured trails flowing across the warm ivory ground toward the foreground, tiny scattered cobalt pigment particles throughout the landscape, small warm-ivory crescent moon positioned high in the upper-right sky, strict limited three-color palette of saturated cobalt blue, warm ivory/off-white and black only, flat graphic screen-print aesthetic, distressed risograph texture, coarse stippled grain, visible paper fibers, uneven ink density, imperfect ink registration, rough hand-printed edges, slightly faded vintage print quality, subtle halftone texture, strong minimalist composition, enormous negative space, strange poetic atmosphere, experimental 1990s art-book editorial design, sophisticated surrealism, tactile handmade print feeling, no gradients, no realistic lighting, no extra colors, no buildings, no people, no vegetation, no text, no circles, no CGI, no 3D render, no polished digital illustration, no photorealism.
```

## variation rules

- Preserve the subject scale and isolation when changing framing, density or mark direction.
- If the user asks for a new object, replace the selected scene only when they clearly request a fourth motif. Keep the same three-color palette, print process, negative space and anti-circle constraint.
- Treat “circular burst” in scene 03 as a loose radial field, never as a closed circle, halo or geometric sun.
- Treat “rainfall” in scene 01 as pigment trails, not realistic weather.
- Treat the blue movement in scene 02 as tall vertical waves, not rings or spirals.
